The SIMAS Programming Language.

The most powerful assembly-like language to date.
SIMAS (SIMple ASsembly) is a high-level yet low-level-feeling language, created by the Turrnut Open Source Organisation.
In this page, you will find out just how amazing the SIMAS Programming Language truly is.

Why pick SIMAS?

Speed.

SIMAS, with all its simplicity, comes a lot of speed. SIMAS is a fast-running language that not only runs quickly, but is also quick to get using.

Ease of use.

SIMAS is one of the simplest languages out there, with a stupidly simple syntax and an instruction set understandable to almost anybody.

Educational.

Not only is SIMAS a pleasant language to use, but it's worth noting the education value it brings, with its assembly-like syntax and behaviours being perfect for someone who wants to get into assembly.
Not only that, but SIMAS also holds educational value in the sense that you can gain tremendous amounts of experience by writing your very own SIMAS implementation.

Choice.

SIMAS is a wide language, with multiple different implementations to choose from.
Your aren't just stuck choosing between two different options, as SIMAS offers many different choices to cater to your own needs.
Want to integrate SIMAS in the web? Choose SIMASJS! Want low-level speed and tight performance? CMAS! Want a user-friendly GUI? Objective-CMAS!
There's an implementation for everything, and at least one of them has your name on it.

Support.

SIMAS is an actively developed language, with maintenance and new updates constantly.
SIMAS will be there whenever you need it, at your beck and call, and never stale.
